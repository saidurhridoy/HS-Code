Privacy Policy — DutyLens BD

Effective Date: October 26, 2026  |  Application: DutyLens BD (Web & Android App com.dutylensbd.app)

1. Overview

DutyLens BD provides AI-assisted Harmonized System (WCO HS2022) tariff classification, Bangladesh Customs (NBR) import duty/VAT calculation, tax rebate simulation, zero-tax pathway discovery, and destination port analysis.

2. Camera & Photo Upload Permission

When you choose to use the optional Camera / Photo Upload feature, the app requests access to your device camera or selected image file solely to inspect product packaging, spec plates, or labels for HS code classification. Photos are processed transiently in memory for classification and are never sold or shared for advertising.

3. Account & Saved Query Data

If you create an optional trader profile, we store your Full Name, Company Name, Business Role (Importer/Exporter), Mobile Number, Email Address, hashed password (scrypt), and your saved HS classification queries in an encrypted cloud PostgreSQL database (Supabase) so you can revisit your history across devices. You may delete saved queries at any time from your Profile panel.

4. Optional Third-Party AI Keys

If you voluntarily configure your own API key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq) in AI Engine Settings, your key is stored locally on your device (localStorage) and transmitted only to the selected AI provider to fulfill your classification request.

5. Data Security & Deletion Requests

All traffic between the DutyLens BD mobile/web application and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS. Users may delete saved query records directly inside the app or request complete account deletion by contacting the publisher.
